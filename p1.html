Sri Lanka is a diverse island nation known as the "Pearl of the Indian Ocean," offering tropical beaches, tea plantations, ancient ruins, and wildlife. Key attractions include Sigiriya Lion Rock, the Cultural Triangle, Kandy's Temple of the Tooth, and beaches in Bentota and Mirissa. The best time to visit is typically December to April.
