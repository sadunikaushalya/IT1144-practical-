To effectively describe a person, you should organize your details into structured layers, moving from observable physical traits to internal personality characteristics and behavior. Building a description in a logical order helps your reader or listener create a clear, vivid mental image. ABA English 1. Physical Appearance (What They Look Like) Start with the general silhouette before moving to specific facial features. ABA English Height and Build: Use specific adjectives like tall, petite, athletic, stocky, or slender. Hair: Detail the length, texture, and color, such as shoulder-length curly brown hair or cropped blonde hair. Facial Features: Mention eye color, face shape, or unique characteristics like freckles, dimples, or glasses.
